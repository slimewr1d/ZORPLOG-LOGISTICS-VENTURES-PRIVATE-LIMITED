FAQ

Good questions.

Who can use ZORPLOG's warehousing?

Startups, SMEs, D2C and e-commerce brands, importers, manufacturers and enterprises across fashion, beauty, home and living, electronics, automotive, healthcare and more.

What is included in your 3PL service?

Receiving, quality checks, put-away, storage, order processing, pick and pack, labelling, dispatch, transport coordination, returns handling and reporting.

How much space do you have?

The facility is about 50,000 sq ft with roughly 45,000 sq ft of storage, 9,000+ pallet positions and 10+ dock doors. Current availability is confirmed when you enquire.

Can you integrate with my systems?

Yes. We integrate with ERPs such as SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics, Tally and Zoho, as well as custom platforms, and give you a customer portal for live inventory and order visibility.

What are your operating hours?

Warehouse operations run 24×7. The office is open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:30 PM, and WhatsApp is the fastest way to reach the team.

Is it suitable for early-stage startups?

Yes. The startup logistics program offers pay-as-you-grow storage, inventory support and technology-enabled operations, so you don't need to build a warehouse or logistics team.

How do I get started?

Message us on WhatsApp or visit the facility. We begin with a scoped conversation about your SKU count, order profile, geography and integration needs.
